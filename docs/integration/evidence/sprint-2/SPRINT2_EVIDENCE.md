## ML-02 / ML-03 / ML-06 — empirical experiments (READY, waiting on one input)

`ml-service/scripts/sprint2_experiments.py` implements the binding protocol:
* E1–E5 (E4 at synthetic weights 0.3 and 0.5) on one stratified 70/15/15 empirical split.
* Tuning by stratified 5-fold cross-validation on the training fold only (40 candidates).
* Pre-registered selection: holdout macro F1, ties broken by minimum per-class F1.
* SHAP and full provenance written into `model_metadata.json`.
* Output to `ml-service/artifacts/v2/`. v1 is never overwritten.

Mechanics test (`ml-service/scripts/test_sprint2_experiments.py`, 5 tests, on a schema fixture):
* All six experiments run.
* The selection rule is applied correctly.
* Zero holdout rows are used in training.
* The output **passes `audit_leakage.py` 6/6** (v1 passes 3/6).
* The artifact serves the frozen vector.
* With the fixture v2 loaded through `SWDI_MODEL_PATH` / `MODEL_METADATA_PATH`, `test_int01_ml_contract.js`
  passes (p95 24.4 ms).
